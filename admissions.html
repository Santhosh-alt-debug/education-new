Process

What happens after you apply

Four steps from form to first class

  1. Apply online

    Create an account and fill in the application. It takes about ten minutes.

  2. Aptitude check

    A 40-minute online test within three days. Results arrive the same evening.

  3. Counsellor call

    We confirm your course, batch and any scholarship on a 20-minute call.

  4. Pay and join

    Pay in full or the first instalment. Your welcome kit arrives a week before class.

    Admission questions

    Answers before you apply

    Common questions about admissions

    Is there an application fee?

    No. Applying and taking the aptitude check are free. You pay only after the counsellor call, once you are sure.

    What if I fail the aptitude check?

    You can retake it once after seven days. Most learners who retake it pass after reviewing the free practice set.

    Can I get a refund?

    Yes. Leave within the first 14 days of class and we refund 90 percent. After that, fees are not refundable, but you can defer to a later batch once.

    Do you offer education loans?

    We work with two lenders who offer loans at 11 to 13 percent interest. The counsellor can share details; we do not earn a commission on them.
