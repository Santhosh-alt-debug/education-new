Terms and conditions for learners and visitors

The rules for enrolling, paying, attending and completing a course at Stackly Academy.

Agreement

When these terms apply

Agreeing to these terms

These terms apply when you create an account, apply for a course or attend a class at Stackly Academy.

By ticking "I accept the terms" when creating an account, you agree to them. Where these terms and a written offer letter differ, the offer letter applies.

Enrolment

Securing a seat

Enrolment and seats

A seat is confirmed only after the aptitude check, the counsellor call and the first payment.

Classroom batches are capped at 45 learners. If a batch fills, we offer the next batch or live online study at the online fee.

We may move a batch start by up to 14 days with at least seven days of notice.

Fees

Paying for a course

Fees and instalments

Fees are shown in rupees and include GST. Instalments are due on the dates in your offer letter.

If an instalment is more than 15 days late, access to classes and recordings pauses until it is paid.

Scholarships are confirmed in writing before payment and cannot be combined except for the alumni referral.

Refunds

If you leave

Refunds and deferrals

If you leave within 14 days of the first class, we refund 90 percent of fees paid.

After 14 days, fees are not refundable, but you may defer once to a batch starting within six months.

If we cancel a batch, we refund all fees paid within 10 working days.

Conduct

Learning together

Conduct and academic honesty

Submitted projects must be your own work. Code or designs from other sources must be credited.

Copying another learner's graded project leads to a failed module the first time and removal from the course the second time.

Harassment of any kind leads to removal without refund.

Certificates

Completing the course

Certificates and placement support

You receive a certificate when you pass every graded project and attend at least 80 percent of sessions.

Placement support is a service, not a guarantee of employment. Outcomes depend on the job market and on your own effort.

Questions about these terms can be sent to admissions@stacklyacademy.in.
