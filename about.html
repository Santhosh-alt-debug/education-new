Our story

Why a software company runs a school

We could not hire so we started teaching

In 2019 Stackly Technologies had twelve open roles and could not fill them. Graduates from nearby colleges were bright but had never used Git, written a test or worked in a team.

So four of our engineers started a weekend class. Twenty-two people came. Nine joined Stackly; the rest were hired elsewhere within months. That class became Stackly Academy.

Milestones

From meeting room to campus

How we got here

  1. 2019

    Started inside Stackly Technologies

    Four engineers taught a weekend React class to 22 learners in a meeting room.

  2. 2021

    First full-time batches

    Full-stack and Java programmes launched with a dedicated floor and 60 lab seats.

  3. 2023

    Design and business streams

    UI/UX, data analytics and digital marketing joined, taught by working practitioners.

  4. 2025

    Live online classes

    Every course opened to learners across Tamil Nadu with the same mentors and reviews.

  5. 2026

    1,240 learners placed

    We passed the milestone in August, with graduates in 60 partner companies.
