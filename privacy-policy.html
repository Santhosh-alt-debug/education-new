Privacy

Last updated 1 September 2026

Privacy policy how we handle your data

What we collect when you enquire, apply or study with us, why we collect it and how long we keep it.

Scope

Who this policy covers

Who this policy applies to

This policy covers visitors to this website, people who enquire or apply, current learners and graduates of Stackly Academy, a unit of Stackly Technologies Private Limited, Salem.

It does not cover websites of hiring partners or lenders we mention. Those organisations have their own policies.

If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must submit enquiries and applications on your behalf.

Collection

What we ask for and why

Information we collect

When you enquire we collect your name, email, phone number, pincode, age, course of interest and your message. We use these only to reply and to suggest a batch.

When you apply we also collect your education details, aptitude test results and identity documents needed to issue a certificate.

  • Contact details: to reply and send class updates
  • Education details: to check eligibility
  • Payment records: to issue receipts and GST invoices
  • Class attendance and project grades: to run the course and issue certificates

Use

How the information is used

How we use your data

We use your information to run your course, answer questions, process payments and, if you opt in, introduce you to hiring partners.

We never sell your information. We do not use it for advertising by third parties.

With your written consent, we may quote your feedback on our website using your first name and course.

Sharing

Who else sees it

When we share information

Hiring partners see your CV and project portfolio only after you opt into placement support and approve each introduction.

Payment processors receive the details needed to take a payment. Our accountants see payment records for tax filings.

We share information with authorities only when the law requires it.

Retention

How long we keep it

Keeping and deleting data

Enquiries that do not become applications are deleted after 12 months.

Learner records, grades and certificates are kept for 10 years so we can verify certificates for employers.

Payment records are kept for eight years, as Indian tax law requires.

Your rights

What you can ask us to do

Your rights and how to use them

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we are not required to keep it.

Write to privacy@stacklyacademy.in or visit the admissions office. We reply within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
