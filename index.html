How it works

Four steps from enquiry to offer letter

From first call to first salary

  1. Talk to a counsellor

    A 20-minute call to match your background to a course. No fee, no pressure.

  2. Take the aptitude check

    A 40-minute online test of logic and English. It sets your batch, not your admission.

  3. Learn by building

    Weekly projects reviewed by a mentor, with a demo day at the end of every month.

  4. Interview with partners

    Mock interviews, a reviewed CV, then introductions to hiring partners who fit.

Before you sign up

Do I need a computer science degree?

No. About 40 percent of our learners come from mechanical, civil, commerce or arts backgrounds. You need a completed degree or diploma and the aptitude check.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Every course can be paid in three instalments with no interest. Paying in full upfront takes 5 percent off.

Are classes in English or Tamil?

Teaching and material are in English. Mentors switch to Tamil in doubt sessions whenever it helps.

What if I miss a class?

Every session is recorded and posted within three hours. You can also join the same topic in another batch that week.
